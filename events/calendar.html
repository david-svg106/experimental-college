Discussion Space with Topic on San Francisco Department of Public Health Mishandling of Hunterspoint Radiation

Date: December 17th, 2026

Time: TBD

Location: On campus, San Francisco State TBD

Former '68 Strikers, Ray Tompkins, Steve Zeltzer, Joel Eis will organize a discussion space to speak on SF DPH's deliberate ignorance over the health concerns of District 10.

Once home to the former Navy Shipyard, Hunterspoint has been a depot for industrial pollution and waste dating back to the 1870s inception of "Butchertown." Post WWII atomic bomb

testing in the pacific has lead to irradiated particulate matter pollution in the air and surfaces of District 10. District 10 has historically housed low-income

Black, Pacific Islander, and people of color from financial policy ranging back from redlining and historical industrial zoning.

This event shall be hosted by the Experimental College secretary@experimentalcollege.com
